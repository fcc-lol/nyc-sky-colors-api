Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version

NYC Sky Colors publishes the color of the sky over New York City. It has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. It does not ask for personal information and does not sell or share any.

This policy covers the website at nyc-sky-colors.fcc.lol, its JSON API and its MCP server. The service is run by FCC.

What the service collects

  • No personal data. There is no sign-up, login or form. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • Requests. The application does not record who asks for sky colors or what they ask for. A request carries only what is needed to answer it: optionally a date and a time.
  • Server logs. Like most websites, the server that hosts this service may keep standard access logs, which can include your IP address, browser or client name, the address requested and the time. These are used only to keep the service running and to investigate abuse.

Using the MCP server with an AI assistant

When an assistant such as Claude calls one of the MCP tools, this service receives only the tool call itself: the tool name and, for historical lookups, a date and an optional time. It does not receive your conversation, your name, your account details or anything else about you.

The assistant you use is operated by another company, and its own privacy policy governs how it handles your conversations and the results this service returns.

What the service stores

The only data this service keeps is the sky colors themselves: four hex color codes for every 15-minute sample, with the date and time they were taken. The colors are averaged from patches of open sky in a public webcam stream of the New York skyline. No images or video are kept, and the data contains nothing about any person.

Third parties

  • Sky colors are sampled from EarthCam's public live stream of SUMMIT One Vanderbilt. That request is made by the server, not by your browser.
  • The site links to EarthCam and to Claude. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.
  • The pages load no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
